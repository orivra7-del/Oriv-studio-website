05 // RETENTION & SECURITY

Data Retention & Security Safeguards

Data Retention Policy (DPDP Section 8(7))

In accordance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, personal data is not retained longer than necessary to satisfy the purpose of collection:

  • Prospective Enquiries: Inquiries and related communications are retained for up to 12 months following the date of last communication, unless a longer retention period is required for legal, regulatory, tax, or legitimate business purposes, after which they are permanently deleted from our mailbox.
  • Active Client Engagements: Personal data relating to contracted clients is retained for the active project duration plus statutory tax and accounting retention requirements under Indian law (e.g. Income Tax Act, 1961).

Security Measures & Technical Safeguards

We maintain rigorous administrative and technical security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss of personal data:

  • Website traffic is served over HTTPS using TLS encryption provided by our hosting platform (Vercel).
  • Enquiry emails are accessed via authenticated accounts protected by standard security credentials and multi-factor authentication (MFA/2FA).
  • Our static website architecture eliminates server-side database vulnerabilities (such as SQL injection or credential leaks).

Please note: While we implement reasonable technical safeguards to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely infallible, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

06 // YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS

Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

Under Sections 11, 12, 13, and 14 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

✦ Right to Access (Sec. 11)
Request a summary of your personal data processed by us and the identities of any Data Processors with whom it was shared.
✦ Right to Correction (Sec. 12)
Request the correction, completion, or updating of any inaccurate, misleading, or incomplete personal data.
✦ Right to Erasure (Sec. 12)
Request the complete deletion of your personal data where retention is no longer necessary for the original enquiry purpose.
✦ Right to Withdraw Consent (Sec. 6)
Withdraw your consent at any time as easily as giving it, without impacting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
✦ Right to Grievance Redressal (Sec. 13)
Access an accessible and readily available grievance redressal mechanism regarding our data processing practices.
✦ Right to Nominate (Sec. 14)
Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the unfortunate event of death or incapacity.
